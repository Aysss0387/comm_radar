摘要：During emergency crises, the rapid spread of social media rumours can threaten public safety and social stability. Although users can play an important role in countering misinformation, user-led rumour refutation remains underexplored as a prosocial digital behaviour. This study develops an integrated psychosocial framework that combines the Theory of Planned Behaviour (TPB) and the Norm Activation Model (NAM) to explain the rational and moral drivers of the intention to refute rumours. Data from 565 active social media users were analysed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modelling (PLS-SEM) in SmartPLS 4.0.
