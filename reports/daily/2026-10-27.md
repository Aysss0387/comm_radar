摘要：The widespread integration of artificial intelligence (AI)–driven communication agents, particularly large language models, has brought about a paradigm shift in communication research. Traditional human-centered paradigms are becoming inadequate for analyzing the behaviors and impacts of these non-human agents, which exhibit autonomous and intelligent communicative capabilities. This article advocates for the development of a Machine Behavior Paradigm in communication studies. This proposed paradigm explicitly expands the disciplinary scope by integrating machine behavior into three core dimensions. About research objects, it emphasizes the shifts from a human-only focus to a dual focus on humans and intelligent machines as communicative subjects.
