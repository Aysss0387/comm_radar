The study is guided by five research questions addressing the definition and propagation of health misinformation, the mechanics of the Causal Story Framework, and the political, social, and remedial consequences of politicized misinformation. The review indicates that health misinformation spreads through algorithmic amplification, trusted social networks, and deliberate fabrication by motivated actors, and that its persistence rests on simplicity, emotional appeal, and confirmation bias.
